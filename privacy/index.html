What I collect, and why

Your email address, when you ask for the book.

I collect it so I can send you the book and, if you confirmed that you wanted them, occasional emails about documentation systems. I also keep a record of when you confirmed and the network address you confirmed from, because I have to be able to show that you agreed rather than assert it.

The lawful basis is your consent. You gave it by confirming your address, and you can withdraw it at any time without giving a reason.

What I record about emails. I record whether a link in an email was clicked. I do not track whether an email was opened, which means no invisible pixel and no record of when you read anything.

What the book page counts. Nothing yet. When counting starts, this page will say what is counted before it begins, and it will be four things and nothing else: that the page was opened, the furthest chapter reached in a visit, whether the PDF was downloaded, and whether a link to my audit service was followed. Each stored as a number with a date and no identifier, never connected to your email address.

What your browser remembers. The book page saves your reading position in your own browser so you can return to where you left off. It stays on your device, it is never sent to me, and clearing your browser data removes it.

What I do not collect. I do not store IP addresses apart from the one in your consent record, and I do not store device fingerprints. There is no advertising network here and nothing on this site follows you anywhere else.

How long I keep it

Your email address: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.

Your consent record: for [three years] after you unsubscribe. It holds the time you confirmed and the network address the confirmation came from, and nothing else. That address is the evidence that it was you, and it is used for nothing else. I keep it after you leave for one reason, which is that if anyone ever asks me to prove you had agreed, the record is the only thing that can.

What you can ask me to do

Withdrawing consent does not undo what was lawful before you withdrew it, and it does not take the book away.

Email me and I will action it. I will not ask why, and I will not try to talk you out of it. If I have not replied within thirty days, that is a failure on my part and you are entitled to say so to the authority below.
